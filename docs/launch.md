# Launch

Locked on 31 October 2026.

## When it goes live

The first face is someone people are already arguing about that week. A quiet name on a random Monday is a weak start. The day is chosen so the countries the maker can actually reach are awake. The maker stays online for the first changes at the top of the board.

## What gets posted

The first post is one screenshot and one sentence: one face, and which country is punching it more.

After that, the maker posts again when the leading country changes. Those updates are the campaign. There is no ad spend and no boosted post.

## The paid month

A promoted launch week uses the Workers paid plan, turned on before the post. The free plan stops the API at 100,000 requests per UTC day, which would freeze the shared count during a busy day. The $5 month is only for a launch the maker is about to promote, or for a later month they choose. Ko-fi tips can cover it.

The in-app pause still exists. If the API stops, the face keeps reacting on the phone and the country scores wait.

## What this is not

This is not a paid-placement launch. Visitors do not pay to rank, and punching does not buy them an audience. The thing worth sharing is the country race and the face.
