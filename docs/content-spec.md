# Content spec

Locked on 31 October 2026.

## The bench

MVP1 needs a bench of about fifteen to twenty-five faces, recorded in the [bench](roster.md). One of them is on the site at a time. The bench is the pool for about a year. Names can be reused after they have been off the site.

The bench has a working list of 25 names. The maker updates it from the news before a face goes live. The old draft of five famous names per country is retired. Those names are not the cast.

## Who can be on the bench

A face is a living public figure that a wide audience already knows and that a large share of that audience is willing to punch.

A loved public figure is not eligible. National sports heroes and widely adored artists are the usual miss. Recognition is not enough.

The useful pool is smaller than a list of famous people. It is mostly polarizing leaders known outside their own country, global business figures people already argue about, and celebrities who are famous and already disliked. The maker chooses the final bench. Visitors cannot add a name.

## The picture

The live face has one still cartoon. The punch, the squash, and the flash are motion in the interface, applied to that still. There is no second drawing for the hit, and no frame sequence.

The drawing is slapstick. It reads as that person at a glance. It is not a photograph, not a traced photo, and not gore.

The first approved cartoon is the standard for the later ones. Only the live face has to be drawn before launch. The rest of the bench can be drawn as each face is about to go up.

Store one image per face. A small WebP is the expected file.

## Changing the face

The maker watches the weekly count. While it is moving, the face stays. When it goes quiet, the maker picks the next name from the bench, replaces the image, and redeploys.

That redeploy clears the weekly country board and leaves the all-time country board. The disclaimer on the page is the public version of this rule.
