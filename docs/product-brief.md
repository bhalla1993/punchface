# Product brief

Locked on 31 October 2026.

## What it is

punchface.click is a public website. One cartoon face is on screen for the whole world. A person punches it to let off steam. Their punches count for their country. The board shows which countries are punching more.

The face is a living public figure people are willing to hit. Loved public figures are not used. The drawing is slapstick. The site is for anyone, including people who will only come once.

## The promise

You land on one familiar face, you hit it, and you see your country move on a shared board.

## Who it is for

A normal visitor with a phone and a spare minute. No account. No instructions beyond the face and the country board.

The maker’s aim is a product people actually use. Recognition comes from the site being public and memorable.

## The visit

1. The current face is already on screen and can be punched immediately.
2. One tap adds one punch for the visitor’s own country, until that address has used 500 punches for the day.
3. After 500, the face still moves. Those hits stay on the phone. A short line says they no longer add to the count.
4. A visitor in one of the 23 launch countries scores for that country. Everyone else scores for Rest of world.
5. The weekly country board is the main race. The all-time country board sits beside it.
6. The visitor’s country stays visible even when it is outside the leading rows.
7. A shared link opens the current face. Punches still count for the visitor’s own country.

## What the product refuses

- Signup, login, and ads
- Photos of real people, gore, and frame-by-frame punch animations
- A different face per country, and a switcher that moves punches onto another country
- Visitors adding their own person
- A paywall, including selling extra punches. A Ko-fi link sits in the footer

## Name

The site name is punchface.click.

## Language

The interface is English. The face’s name is written the way the public already knows it.

## Sound

Sound stays off until the visitor turns it on.
