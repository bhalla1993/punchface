# Bench

Working list for review on 31 October 2026. These are political figures a large audience outside their own base is already willing to punch. Loved athletes and celebrity rivalries are not on this list.

One face is live at a time. The rest wait. A name can be used again later. Mark the launch face when you choose it. Cross out anyone you do not want.

Ali Khamenei is not listed. He died in 2026. Mojtaba Khamenei is the current supreme leader.
