Privacy

This page says what the site keeps. There is no account.

Your network address

When a punch counts, your network address is stored with the number of punches that address has used that UTC day. It is kept only to enforce the daily limit. It is deleted at the end of the UTC day. People on the same network share that limit. The address is not shown on the board, and it is not sold.

Your country

The country comes from the network, so a punch can score for that country. The board keeps country totals for this week and for all time. Those totals are not a list of people. Changing the face clears the weekly totals and leaves the all-time totals.

The browser check

Before a punch counts, Cloudflare may check the browser. That check can include the network address, and Cloudflare may use a cookie for it. If the check does not pass, the punch does not count. The face can still move.

Who serves the page

Cloudflare serves the site. They see the connection that delivers the page, including the network address and the country.

The type on the page is loaded from Google Fonts. Opening the page asks Google for those font files.

Other sites

GitHub, the portfolio, and Ko-fi are other sites. A tip is handled by Ko-fi. This site does not see your payment.

What this site does not do

No account. No ads. No mailing list. The site does not sell information. It does not set a cookie to remember you.
