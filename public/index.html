    How it works

    1. 1

      Punch the face. Click or tap. The face reacts right away. Sound stays off until you turn it on in the menu.

    2. 2

      Your country scores. Punches stick to the country you are visiting from. You cannot hand them to another country.

    3. 3

      Watch the board. This week is the race and resets Monday. All time keeps the total.

    The face is chosen by the site and replaced when punching slows. This is a game, not a claim of fact.

    A few more rules

    • One tap is one punch. A shake or a combo does not add extra points.
    • 500 punches a day add to the board. After that you can keep punching for fun. Those hits no longer add to the count.
    • Monday at midnight UTC starts a new week. The face does not change just because the week did.
    • The face is chosen by the site and replaced when punching slows.
